What it does

Built for game day desktops

Keep the slate in the corner of your eye while you work, stream, or watch.

  • Docked AppBar

    Reserves the top of the screen so windows don’t hide under the strip. Float it if you prefer.

  • Multi-display

    Single, clone, or continuous span across the monitors you pick.

  • Live detail

    Scores, down & distance, last play, QB stats, ball-on, possession, and scoring flash.

  • Leaders

    Weekly and season PASS / RUSH / REC leaders — jump to them with L when they’re on the ticker.
