NFL-TCKR CHANGELOG
==================

0.1.47 — 2026-09-29
-------------------
- Scroll: passer-line rotation on a final (and live) card stamps that line
  onto the cached card. The names, logos, and team-name glow are not
  repainted every 5 seconds.

0.1.46 — 2026-09-29
-------------------
- Scroll: team-name glow stays on the cached card. Live ticks still stamp
  only the clock, down, play, score, timeouts, and possession icon. Glow-all
  still repaints the whole card.

0.1.45 — 2026-09-29
-------------------
- Scroll: cache the stable part of each live card (names, logos) and stamp
  only the clock, down, play, score, timeouts, and possession icon so a
  tick does not rebuild the whole card on the GUI thread. Both possession
  slots stay reserved so a possession flip does not change card width.

0.1.44 — 2026-09-29
-------------------
- Scroll: paint changed cards on the GUI thread one per event-loop turn
  (QTimer.singleShot(0)); install each image as it finishes so VBlank wakes
  are not starved by a QThread holding the GIL in QPainter

0.1.43 — 2026-09-29
-------------------
- Live cards: reserve center from clock + fixed down samples only; last play /
  drive wraps up to 3 lines inside that column so scores sit closer

0.1.42 — 2026-09-29
-------------------
- Scroll: yield the GIL during card rebuilds and install only finished images so
  down / play / score updates do not drop a VBlank frame

0.1.41 — 2026-09-29
-------------------
- Scoring flash: 3s all-caps type line (e.g. GIANTS TOUCHDOWN!), then 4s play detail
- Detail form e.g. Giants Score: Scattebo 22 YD Run Touchdown!; hold is 7s total

0.1.40 — 2026-09-29

0.1.31 — 2026-09-29
-------------------
- Glow all: keep halos cached (text, logos, possession icons) and drop the

  halo on the next card and holding the scroll clock for hundreds of ms
  per card; the scroll blit itself stayed under 1ms
- Card rebuild runs at low priority and yields between halos so a cold glow
  pass cannot pin the machine
